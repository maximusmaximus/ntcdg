Make a tarot deck that exists nowhere else — and give it away.

NTCDG helps artists create a complete, one-of-a-kind tarot deck: original art for every card, a written meaning for each one, and print-ready sheets with a QR code on every back.

These decks are made to be given. Each card is created for, and handed to, one particular person. Its energy is just for them — when they scan the back, they read what their card holds, privately, in their own time.

How it works

  1. 1 · Dream it

    Describe a vibe and theme. Choose a full 78-card deck, the 22 Major Arcana, or a custom size.

  2. 2 · Bring your symbols

    Upload your own drawings of traditional symbols. The rest of the deck is generated in your style.

  3. 3 · Watch it appear

    See every prompt, every meaning and every image as it is created — nothing is hidden.

  4. 4 · Print & give

    Duplex-registered sheets with a personal QR on each back. Cut, shuffle, and hand each card to its person.

Scanned a card?

The QR code on the back of your card opens its own page: the card's image and everything it represents, from its symbols to its upright and reversed meanings. Swipe to wander through the rest of the deck, or scan another card.
