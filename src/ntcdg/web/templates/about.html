About NTCDG

Decks made to be given away

NTCDG — the Novel Tarot Card Deck Generator — exists for one purpose: to help you make a tarot deck that has never existed before, and then to give its cards away, one by one, to the people they are for.

Each card belongs to one person

An NTCDG deck is not a fortune-telling kit for the maker. Each card is created for, and handed to, a particular person. The energy of each card is just for that person — it is theirs alone. It is not meant to be read by the maker as a fortune for others, and it is not a verdict handed down from above. It is a gift.

As the maker, you are a channel and a gift-giver. You shape the deck, you choose its symbols and its mood, and then you let each card go. Who receives which card is part of the magic: the card finds its person, and what it means is between the card and them.

A private page behind every card

Every printed card carries a small QR code on its back. When the person you gave it to scans it, their phone opens that card's own page: the card's image, its title and arcana, the symbols woven into it, a description, and its upright and reversed meanings. They can read it privately, in their own time — tonight, next year, whenever the card asks to be understood. They can swipe through the rest of the deck, or scan another card if someone else shares theirs.

The link is permanent. The deck's web address is baked in the first time it is printed, so cards already given away keep working even when you reprint.

The structure of tarot

A traditional deck has 78 cards in two parts:

  • The Major Arcana — 22 cards, from 0 The Fool to XXI The World: the great archetypes and turning points of a life's journey.
  • The Minor Arcana — 56 cards in four suits: Wands (fire, will, creativity), Cups (water, feeling, love), Swords (air, thought, truth) and Pentacles (earth, body, work and resources). Each suit runs Ace to Ten, plus four court cards: Page, Knight, Queen and King.

Every card has an upright meaning and a reversed meaning — the same energy turned inward, blocked, or asking for attention. NTCDG writes both for every card, so whoever receives it can read whichever way it came to them.

Your symbols, your style

Tarot speaks through symbols: the Fool's small dog and cliff edge, the Star's water pitchers, the Moon's twin towers. NTCDG knows the canonical, traditional symbols of every card. Artists can draw their own versions of these symbols and upload them; NTCDG matches them to the traditional archetypes, tells you which ones are still missing, and generates the rest of the deck in your style. Or let it generate a cohesive set of symbols for you.

AI as an assistant to the artist

The art and text are created with Venice.ai, a privacy-focused AI platform, using your own Venice API key (stored encrypted on your profile; it is never shared, and nobody else's key is ever used for your decks). The AI helps the artist — it does not replace them. You choose the vision, the symbols, the words you keep and the cards you give. Everything is transparent: you can see every prompt sent and every result returned as your deck is made.

Printing

When your deck is ready, NTCDG produces print-ready PDFs at the true 2.75″ × 4.75″ tarot size with bleed, in CMYK, laid out so that every back lines up exactly with its front when printed double-sided. A calibration sheet helps you correct any drift your printer has. A companion booklet holds every card's meanings.
